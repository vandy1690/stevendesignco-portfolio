## Two things, one page

Quoting someone takes two different forms on this site and they are documented
together because the rules on the words are identical. They are not the same
markup.

1. **The recommendation card**, a `figure.sdc-quote` with a decorative quote mark, a
   blockquote and a figcaption holding the speaker's title. Fixed size, so a row
   of them can be fanned. It is page-level markup in `src/pages/index.astro` and
   there is no component for it.
2. **The case study pull quote**, a bare `blockquote.sdc-cs-quote` set larger than the
   prose around it. This one is the `Quote` component, and it carries no caption.

Everything below about the words applies to both. Everything about captions and
fixed height applies to the card only.

## In code

`src/components/ui/Quote.astro` is **the case study pull quote**, not the fan
card. The words go in the slot.

```astro
---
import Quote from '../../components/ui/Quote.astro';
---
<Quote>
	Naming is the part that decides whether anybody else can use it.
</Quote>
```

No props. It carries no attribution line, which is why the caption rules below
do not apply to it: only use it where the prose around it has already said who is
speaking.

## The rules on the words

**Verbatim or not at all.** One sentence, copied exactly from the source.
Trimming for length is allowed; rewording is not, including fixing grammar.

**Titles, never names.** On the fan card, the caption is the person's role and
company, such as "Product Designer, PayPal Merchant Enablement". Naming people
turns a portfolio into a reference list they did not agree to. The pull quote has
no caption, so the attribution goes in the prose above it, under the same rule.

**Nothing the rest of the site cannot back up.** A quote is not a loophole for a
claim that is otherwise barred. If a recommender praised a number the site does
not publish, that sentence is not usable.

**No em dashes.** A quoted sentence containing one is re-punctuated or another
sentence is chosen.

## Accessibility

The decorative quote glyph is `aria-hidden`. An automated scan flags it as
visible content hidden from screen readers; that flag is wrong for this case and
should not be "fixed". The mark carries no information the blockquote does not.

## Content length

The fan card is a fixed height. A sentence beyond roughly 180 characters will not
fit at the fan's size. Choose a shorter sentence rather than shrinking the type.
The pull quote is not height constrained, but a pull quote running past two lines
has stopped being a pull quote.

## Checks before you ship

1. The sentence matches the source word for word.
2. No personal name appears, in the card's caption or in a pull quote's lead-in.
3. A fan card's text fits at 1440 and at 390 wide without clipping.
