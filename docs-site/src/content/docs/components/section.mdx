## Measure

Body copy is capped near 800px, not the full 1280px page width. A line of text
longer than about 75 characters is measurably harder to track back from. Use
`.section__inner--narrow` where the content is pure reading.

## Figures

A case study figure sits between the deck and the stats, at full width of the
measure. It used to sit at 75 percent and below the stats; both were changed
because the image is evidence for the claim above it and belongs next to it.

Images are decorative by default: `alt=""`. An image earns alt text only when it
carries information the surrounding copy does not. On this site that is true of
exactly one image, the headshot.

## Captions

`.cs-note` and `.wk-note` are captions: 14px, muted, no rule, no accent bar.
They are for a qualification, a disclosure or a credit. The PayPal proprietary
disclosure is a caption.

A caption is not a pull quote. `.cs-quote` is the display-type treatment for a
sentence worth stopping on. Using the caption style for emphasis makes a
disclosure look like a design flourish.

<div class="sdc-dd">
	<div class="sdc-dd--do">
		<h4>Do</h4>
		<ul>
			<li>Keep reading columns near the narrow measure.</li>
			<li>Put the figure directly under the claim it supports.</li>
			<li>Use a caption for disclosures and credits.</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
	<div class="sdc-dd--dont">
		<h4>Don't</h4>
		<ul>
			<li>Alternate section tone for visual variety.</li>
			<li>Write alt text that repeats the caption.</li>
			<li>Use the caption style to emphasise a sentence.</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
</div>

## Checks before you ship

1. No line of body copy runs much past 75 characters at 1440 wide.
2. Every new image has either empty alt text or a reason for its description.
3. Tone alternation marks a change of idea, not decoration.
