## The scale

Nine steps from 8px to 160px, on tokens. Every gap, padding and section rhythm
comes from it. A value not on the scale is a decision someone will have to
justify later.

## Measures

The page caps at 1280px. Reading columns cap at 800px. The gutter is 24px.

Those two maximums are the whole layout system. A card is the page measure; a
paragraph is the reading measure. Mixing them is what makes a page feel like two
pages.

## Radius

18px for surfaces, 10px for controls. A card and a button should not look like
the same kind of object, and radius is the cheapest way to say so. Buttons used
to be full pills and were changed: a pill reads as a tag or a status, not a
control.

## Elevation

Cards carry a four-stop shadow and deepen on hover. Nothing else on the site is
elevated. If a new element needs a shadow, check first whether it should be a
card.

## The viewport unit that matters

Pinned sections use `svh`, the small viewport height, not `vh`. On mobile
browsers `vh` ignores the address bar, so a full-height section is taller than
the visible window and its content gets cut. This is the single most common
cause of "it looks fine on my laptop" bugs on this site.

## Checks before you ship

1. Every new spacing value is on the scale.
2. Reading columns use the narrow measure, not the page measure.
3. Any full-height section uses svh.
